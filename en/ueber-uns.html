About us

The team behind i.appear

Marilena Tumler

Managing director, editorial lead, UX/UI design and content creation

Marilena guarantees the substantive depth of our projects. As a trained historian, philosopher and media ethicist, she knows exactly how to convey complex history and culture responsibly. She brings this expertise not only to i.appear, but also to her teaching at the FH Vorarlberg University of Applied Sciences in the Design department. She develops the educational concepts for our i.grow education stream and runs workshops where students learn media literacy and the ethical handling of digital content. The entire UX and UI design of i.appear comes from Marilena’s pen. In her role as historian, she is also the gatekeeper for academically grounded content.

This rare combination of humanities depth, media-ethics pedagogy and design practice shapes the signature of i.appear.

Maggy Haidacher

3D, Interactive Systems, Web and AR

Maggy is the interface between system and digital experience. Her geographical background sharpened her eye for spatial relationships early on – today, she’s mostly focused on making complex structures visible and walkable. In her InterMedia master’s programme she specialised in visualising complex systems and 3D modelling; that focus runs through her work, from interactive 3D prototypes to AR applications in public space.

At i.appear she leads the multimedia and technical implementation: from the concept stage through detailed 3D models and animations to the development of interactive systems and the maintenance of the web platform and website.
