Digital Walking Tour Feldkirch

Audio guides and multimedia tours in the medieval Alpine town

Discover Feldkirch digitally: with the walking tours and audio guides by i.appear, you experience architectural heritage, wartime history and media literacy right in the old town – multimedia, interactive and free of charge. Everything runs in your smartphone browser, no app download and no registration.

Feldkirch is a special place for i.appear: this is where school projects are made in which students themselves become the narrators of their city. From democracy and coexistence to the bombings of 1943 – history comes alive in public space.

Walking tours for schools

i.grow – Media literacy in Feldkirch

Zusammenwachsen

A walking tour told as a multimedia experience by students of MS Levis Feldkirch. The young people engage with values, democracy and coexistence in their city – and anchor their stories at real places in Feldkirch. Created within the framework of “Kunst ist Klasse” (BMKOES).

Ein Oktobertag – bombings of 1943

A book and app project on the bombings of Feldkirch during the Second World War. Students of MS Oberau conducted contemporary witness interviews, scoured archives and anchored the stories at the original location – as a digital walking tour with audio guide, historical photographs and augmented reality.

How the digital walking tour works

1. Scan the QR code

On site at the starting point or in advance via the link – the walking tour opens directly in your browser.

2. Discover the locations

Follow the map to the locations. GPS unlocks the content – audio guide, 3D models, AR and videos.

3. Experience and marvel

Immerse yourself in history and culture. No download, no account, no advertising – just set off.
