Digital Walking Tours & Audio Guides

All locations at a glance – in Vorarlberg, the Lake Constance region and beyond

With the digital walking tours and audio guides by i.appear, you discover places in Vorarlberg and the Lake Constance region in a whole new way: multimedia, interactive and free of charge – right in your smartphone browser. At every location, 3D models, augmented reality, audio guide segments, historical photographs and videos are waiting for you.

Our walking tours connect history, culture, tourism and media literacy. Whether a historical journey through time, a regional audio guide experience or an educational project with schools – choose your location and start your tour. No app download, no registration, no advertising.

How a digital walking tour works

1. Scan the QR code

On site at the starting point or in advance via the link – the walking tour opens directly in your browser.

2. Discover the locations

Follow the map to the locations. GPS unlocks the content – audio guide, 3D models, AR and videos.

3. Experience and marvel

Immerse yourself in history and culture. No download, no account, no advertising – just set off.
