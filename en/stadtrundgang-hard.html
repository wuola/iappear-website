Audio guide and multimedia tour along the shore of Lake Constance

Discover Hard on Lake Constance digitally: with the walking tour and audio guide by i.appear, you experience 200 years of local history right on the lake shore – multimedia, interactive and free of charge. Everything runs in your smartphone browser, no app download and no registration.

i.history – Travel through time in Hard

The See Runde – 200 years of local history on Lake Constance

On the See Runde, you set off along the shore of Lake Constance on a journey through the last two centuries of the municipality of Hard. Eight locations lead through the village, complemented by invisible locations that only become visible as you get close.

You hear about the former poorhouse with its farm, the everyday lives of the poorest, the maternity ward and the dissecting house – accompanied by the street sweeper Fiffi, a true Harder original. The digital walking tour links past and present and shows how memory stays alive – across generations and close to the people.

With audio guide, historical photographs, interactive map and augmented reality.

How the digital walking tour works

1. Scan the QR code

On site at the starting point or in advance via the link – the walking tour opens directly in your browser.

2. Discover the locations

Follow the map to the locations. GPS unlocks the content – audio guide, 3D models, AR and videos.

3. Experience and marvel

Immerse yourself in the history of Hard. No download, no account, no advertising – just set off.
