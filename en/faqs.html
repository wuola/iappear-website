Frequently asked questions

FAQs

The questions we hear most

General

What exactly is i.appear?

i.appear is a platform for digital walking tours and audio guides across Vorarlberg. We connect the physical world with digital content to bring places to life. Each walking tour is built from multiple locations with 3D models, augmented reality, audio guides, videos and much more!

Do I need to download an app from the store?

No. i.appear is a progressive web app. You can open it directly through your smartphone browser (e.g. via a QR code on location), without using up storage for a download.

Is it free to use?

Yes. For end users, access to the tours is completely free. Funding comes from the respective municipalities, museums or companies.

Where are tours available?

Currently you’ll find digital walking tours in Dornbirn, Feldkirch, Hard, Au in the Bregenzerwald and other locations across Vorarlberg. The platform is scalable – new regions are added on an ongoing basis.

Usage & technology

Why are some locations locked?

i.appear is location-based. To unlock a location you need to be physically there. Your GPS detects your position and unlocks the content. Once discovered, you can often view the content later from anywhere.

What are the requirements?

All you need is a smartphone with an internet connection and GPS enabled. For AR content, your browser should have access to the camera and an integrated AR Core (a list from Google shows current smartphones with AR Core).

What happens with my location data?

Your location is only used to unlock locations on site. We don’t create movement profiles and we use the data exclusively for the functionality of the tour. We don’t store anything.

Content

What are the three categories?
  • i.history: Historical facts and reconstructions.
  • i.dentity: Culture, region, identity.
  • i.grow: Media literacy, civic education and digital skills – workshops where students design their own digital walking tours.
Who is i.appear for?
  • i.history: For tourists and curious local explorers.
  • i.dentity: Same audience, but regional highlights instead of educational content.
  • i.grow: As an interface for teachers and students in an educational context.

For partners

How do we bring i.appear to our city?

We offer full-service solutions – from concept through 3D content to delivery of the web app. Just get in touch for a consultation.
